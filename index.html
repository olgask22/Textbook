AI for discovering evidence and knowledge

Generative AI is one layer. Six layers do the work.

Each layer has a different job. Chatbots appear in Layer 1 and language models in Layer 3, and neither works without the layers beneath them. Hover over or tap any example for a plain-language explanation.
